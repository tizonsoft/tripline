🧭 Trip Timeline

Turn your trip spreadsheet into a beautiful timeline

Drop in an Excel file with an Itinerary tab (Date, Day, Accommodations, Activities, Notes) and an optional Resources tab of links. Get a phone-friendly day-by-day plan you can print or save as a PDF.

🔒 Nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere — read directly in your browser, forgotten when you close the tab

Expected format

Sheet 1 — Itinerary: one row starts each day, extra rows underneath add more activities to that same day. A blank row separates days.

DateDayAccommodationsActivitiesNotes
26 AugWedHotel Rias BaixasArrive, drive southFlight lands evening
MeisStop in Padrón for dinner

Sheet 2 — Resources (optional): a title row followed by link rows, one group per topic (e.g. a region or hotel).
